# Product Ideas

## Food Discovery Journal

Explore a Pokedex-style collection where meals and ingredients become discoveries. Optional quests can invite players to try foods across different colors or food groups, then add confirmed discoveries to their collection and unlock Nomi/world content.

Design notes:

- Keep quests gentle, flexible, and non-judgmental; no streak penalties or shame for missed quests.
- Let players confirm or correct a discovery when the photo analyzer is unsure.
- Reward variety and curiosity, not eating a prescribed amount or restricting foods.
- Consider culturally diverse foods and accessible alternatives in every quest.
